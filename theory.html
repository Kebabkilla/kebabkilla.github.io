📚 Learn the theory (5-minute lessons)

🎼

1 · Notes & the fretboard

Bass has 4 strings tuned E–A–D–G. Every fret moves up one semitone (half step). Learn the natural notes A–G first, then sharps/flats. Use the Fretboard Trainer above until you can name any fret in under 2 seconds.

Open Fretboard Trainer →

🎵

2 · Scales & modes

A scale is a pattern of whole (W) and half (H) steps. Major = W-W-H-W-W-W-H. Modes are the same notes starting on a different degree — Dorian sounds jazzy, Mixolydian sounds bluesy. Explore them in the Scale Explorer, then drill them with practice3.html.

Open Scale Explorer →

🎹

3 · Chords & extensions

Chords stack thirds: root–3rd–5th. The 3rd decides major vs minor; the 7th adds colour (maj7 = dreamy, dominant 7 = bluesy). Altered dominants like F7alt come from the melodic minor scale — drill them in practice.html.

Open Chord Builder →

📏

4 · Intervals

Intervals are the building blocks of melody and harmony. Perfect 4th/5th/octave, major/minor 2nds, 3rds, 6ths, 7ths. Bassists live on 4ths and 5ths — master those first in the Interval Trainer, then test your ears in the Ear Quiz.

Open Interval Trainer →

🔁

5 · Progressions & rhythm

Songs are chord loops. I–V–vi–IV powers pop; ii–V–I powers jazz. Play them in the Progression Player with the metronome on, then slow the tab player down to 0.5× until the fingering is automatic.

Open Progression Player →

🏋️

6 · How to practise

Short + slow + daily beats long + fast. Loop one bar at 60 BPM, add 5 BPM when clean, practise in all keys, and always play with a metronome. Your existing drills: finger independence · triads.
